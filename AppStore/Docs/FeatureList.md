# AlienContactZone — Feature List

## App Overview
- **App Name**: AlienContactZone
- **Bundle ID**: com.ggsheng.AlienContactZone
- **Core Concept**: A cosmic-themed social exploration app where users can send "signals" into the universe, receive mysterious alien messages, and track their cosmic contact journey. Features beautiful space aesthetics, relaxing soundscapes, and engaging daily challenges.
- **Target Users**: Teens, Young Adults, Adults in Western markets (14-45 years old)
- **App Language**: English (primary), no Chinese characters in UI
- **Platform**: iOS 16.0+
