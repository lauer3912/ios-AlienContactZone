Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 19, 2026

1. Information We Collect

AlienContactZone is designed with your privacy in mind. We collect minimal data necessary to provide our services.

1.1 User-Generated Content

  • Signals: Text content you compose and send as signals is stored locally on your device.
  • Messages: Alien messages you receive are stored locally in your inbox archive.
  • Journey Progress: Your level, achievements, and XP data are stored locally.

1.2 Usage Data

  • App Usage: We track basic app usage (signals sent, messages received, streak days) locally to improve user experience.
  • Achievements: Your badges and progress data are stored locally.

1.3 Device Data

  • Crash Reports: Anonymous crash logs may be collected to help us fix bugs.
  • Widget Data: If you use our widget, anonymous data may be shared for widget functionality.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is processed as follows:

  • Local Storage: All user content (signals, messages, progress) is stored exclusively on your device.
  • No Cloud Storage: We do not store your personal data on external servers.
  • No Third-Party Sharing: We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third parties.
  • Anonymous Analytics: Aggregate, anonymized usage data may be used to improve the app experience.

3. Data Storage and Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:

  • Local-Only Storage: All sensitive user data is stored locally on your device, not on external servers.
  • Secure Deletion: You can delete all your data by uninstalling the app. All local data will be permanently removed.
  • No Account Required: AlienContactZone does not require you to create an account or provide personal information.

4. In-App Purchases

AlienContactZone offers premium subscriptions through the App Store:

  • Payment Processing: All payments are processed securely through Apple's App Store. We never collect your payment information.
  • Purchase History: Your subscription status is managed by Apple and stored locally on your device.
  • Restoration: You can restore purchases using the "Restore Purchase" feature in the app.

5. Children's Privacy

AlienContactZone is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access: You can view all data stored locally by opening the app.
  • Deletion: Uninstalling the app will permanently delete all local data.
  • Privacy Controls: You can manage all privacy settings within the app's Settings screen.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
